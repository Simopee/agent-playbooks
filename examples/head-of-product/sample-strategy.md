# Swapshelf: strategy

> **Status: LOCKED 2026-01-15.** Changes only by the owner's decision. The Head of Product proposes.

## Mission

Swapshelf helps people find the nearest free book-swap shelf and see what is on it before they walk there (owner's words). The core is the map and the shelf page. Everything else, such as accounts and reading lists, is supporting.

## Primary user

A reader on a phone, usually arriving from search ("book swap near me"), who wants to know whether a trip to a shelf is worth it. A smaller group, shelf keepers who update the contents, is valuable but small; features for them are weighed by that size.

## Diagnosis: what is holding us back now (2026-01-10)

Visitors arrive but few come back. Shelf pages are often out of date, so the second visit disappoints (source: 31 of 40 feedback messages mention stale contents, feedback inbox, December). To verify: that stale contents cause the low return rate is an inference; test it by comparing return visits for shelves updated in the last week with the rest, over 8 weeks.

## Guiding policy

Make shelf contents fresh before adding anything new. It follows that: updating a shelf must take under a minute; no new features for readers until freshness improves; growth work waits.

## Bets (January to March 2026)

### Bet 1. One-minute shelf update

- **Metric:** share of shelves updated in the last 7 days. Database, weekly.
- **Actions:** a photo-based update flow for shelf keepers.
- **Kill criterion:** if the share is not above 30% by 2026-03-15, stop and re-evaluate.

### Bet 2. "Last updated" on every shelf page

- **Metric:** return visits within 30 days. Analytics, monthly.
- **Actions:** show the update date; sort stale shelves lower.
- **Kill criterion:** set after the first monthly measurement (2026-02-15).

## Not doing now

1. Reader accounts and reading lists. They serve few users and do not move a bet. Reconsider when the return rate is above 20%.
2. A native app. The web works on phones. Reconsider when a bet shows a need the web cannot meet.

## Decision rules

1. Every piece of work relates to a bet. Otherwise it goes to the idea list.
2. Opportunity cost: is this the best use of the next slot?
3. Decided but not done is raised every time.
4. Kill criterion before starting.
5. For whom and how many.
6. Simplicity: what could be removed instead?
7. A small number is not evidence, and the owner decides.

## Metrics and baselines

| Metric | What it tells | Source and frequency | Baseline (2026-01-10) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shelves updated in last 7 days | Freshness | Database, weekly | 12% |
| Return visits within 30 days | Whether people come back | Analytics, monthly | 9% |
| Shelf keepers active per month | Keeper base | Database, monthly | not measured |

Limits: analytics misses visitors who block tracking; keeper numbers are small, so a change of a few people is not a trend.
